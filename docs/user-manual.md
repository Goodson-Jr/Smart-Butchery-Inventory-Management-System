## Part A — Android app (cashier / attendant)

### A1. Logging in
Open the app, enter your username and password, tap **Log in**.

### A2. Recording a sale
1. Tap **New Sale**.
2. Choose the meat cut.
3. Type the **weight sold** in kg (e.g. `2.5`).
4. The line amount appears (weight × price per kg). Add more cuts if needed.
5. Tap **Complete sale**. Stock is reduced automatically; a confirmation shows the total kg and amount.

### A3. Adding stock (a delivery)
1. Tap **Add Stock**, choose the cut.
2. Enter the delivered weight in kg (and cost per kg if known).
3. Tap **Save**. The cut's available weight goes up.

### A4. Viewing stock
Tap **Stock** to see available kg per cut.

### A5. Today's sales
Tap **Today** to see total kg sold and revenue for the day.

## Part B — Web dashboard (management)

### B1. Logging in
Open the dashboard URL, sign in with a manager account.

### B2. Dashboard
Shows stock per meat type, today's sales (kg and revenue), and any low-stock cuts.

### B3. Meat cuts & prices
Add a meat type; add a cut with its **price per kg** and **low-stock threshold**;
edit a price; retire a cut.

### B4. Stock
Record deliveries and view / adjust current stock.

### B5. Wastage
Record spoiled or expired meat (weight + reason); review wastage over time.

### B6. Reports
Choose a date range and view the sales, profit-estimation and stock-usage reports.

### B7. Users
Create cashier / manager accounts and deactivate ones no longer used.
